About MAGICwhisp

A place to unwind and explore the world of Minecraft, through skins!

Hi there, I'm the creator behind MAGICwhisp as well as a YouTuber, but you can call me MAGIC.
I'm a creator who is obsessed with everything Minecraft, espically skins! Imaginative ideas and building little worlds is just a way I express my love for Minecraft. Here you will find my "scrapbook" of skins and some stories behind the skins that I make.

Early on, when I first played Minecraft several years back, I was so amazed at what the Minecraft Marketplace creators would come up with. Seeing their work, seeing the effort and time, and feeling excited to find my favorite creator had come out with a new pack, always made me appreciate them and love the work. I one day I thought, I want to be able to express my love for skin creation and Marketplace creators by bringing my own work there, showing them: "Hey, look! You inspired me to become one too!" So, I'm now bringing the imagination of everyone to life, from young and old, to enjoy their time on Minecraft in a special skin that feels like it was made just for them!

My main goal is to make sure everyone has that special skin that matches to what they've always been looking for. Cuz, there is always a special pack or single skin that makes your heart jump and you stare at the screen going: "That's the one!" Until I complete that mission, I will continue to create to make them happy. Because happy gamers means happy life!
If you ever want to suggest some ideas, give feedback, or ask for a personal skin, you can contact me at my GitHub profile or email me. Just make sure to check my Contact page for further explaination.

I can't wait to create something amazing for you, your friends and everyone!
